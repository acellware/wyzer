---
name: Patch Management
type: resource-linked
summary: >-
  Whether systems are kept up to date with security patches. Frameworks expect
  known vulnerabilities to be identified and fixed in a timely way.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: nist
    plain: >-
      Vulnerabilities are expected to be identified and platforms kept current so
      known weaknesses are not left exposed.
    detail: >-
      NIST CSF 2.0 Risk Assessment (ID.RA) and Platform Security (PR.PS) cover
      identifying and remediating vulnerabilities.
    citations:
      - label: "NIST CSF 2.0 · ID.RA / PR.PS"
        url: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Vulnerabilities are expected to be identified and remediated, and changes
      managed, to keep systems secure.
    detail: >-
      Maps to CC7.1 (identifying vulnerabilities) and CC8.1 (managing changes,
      including patches).
    citations:
      - label: "SOC 2 TSC · CC7.1 / CC8.1"
        url: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: iso27001
    plain: >-
      Technical vulnerabilities are expected to be identified and addressed in a
      timely manner.
    detail: >-
      Annex A control A.8.8 (management of technical vulnerabilities) in ISO/IEC
      27001:2022.
    citations:
      - label: "ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · A.8.8"
        url: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
  - framework: pci-dss
    plain: >-
      Systems and software must be protected from known vulnerabilities by
      installing applicable security patches.
    detail: >-
      PCI DSS v4.0 Requirement 6 covers developing and maintaining secure systems,
      including timely patching.
    citations:
      - label: "PCI DSS v4.0 · Requirement 6"
        url: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/
---

**In practice.** Keep an inventory of your components, watch for security
advisories, and apply critical patches on a defined timeline, prioritizing
anything internet-facing or handling sensitive data.
