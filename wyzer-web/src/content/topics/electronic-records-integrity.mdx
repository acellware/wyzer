---
name: Electronic Records Integrity
type: industry-only
summary: >-
  Making sure electronic records in FDA-regulated environments are trustworthy.
  21 CFR Part 11 expects controls that keep e-records accurate, complete, and
  tamper-evident.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: fda
    plain: >-
      Regulated electronic records must be produced by validated systems, be
      protected so they can be retrieved accurately, and carry secure, computer-
      generated, time-stamped audit trails of changes.
    detail: >-
      FDA 21 CFR Part 11.10 sets controls for closed systems, including validation
      (11.10(a)), record protection and retrieval (11.10(c)), and audit trails
      (11.10(e)).
    citations:
      - label: "FDA · 21 CFR 11.10(a)(c)(e)"
        url: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11
---

**In practice.** Use validated systems, enforce access controls, and keep
computer-generated, time-stamped audit trails that record who changed what and
when, without overwriting the original values. Be able to produce accurate,
complete copies on request.
