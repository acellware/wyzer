---
name: Electronic Signatures
type: industry-only
summary: >-
  Using electronic signatures in place of handwritten ones in FDA-regulated
  records. 21 CFR Part 11 sets requirements for e-signatures to be legally
  equivalent.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: fda
    plain: >-
      Electronic signatures must be unique to one person, verified before use, and
      permanently linked to their records. Each signed record must show the
      signer's name, the date and time, and the meaning of the signature.
    detail: >-
      FDA 21 CFR Part 11.50 (signature manifestations), 11.70 (signature-to-record
      linking), and 11.100-11.200 (electronic-signature requirements and
      components).
    source: FDA · 21 CFR 11.50 / 11.70 / 11.100
    sourceUrl: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11
---

**In practice.** Bind each signature to its specific record, capture the signer's
identity and their intent (such as approval or authorship), and make sure
signatures cannot be copied, transferred, or reused on other records.
