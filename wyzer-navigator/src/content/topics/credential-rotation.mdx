---
name: Credential & Key Rotation
type: resource-linked
summary: >-
  Whether keys, passwords, and access tokens are rotated and stale credentials
  removed. Frameworks expect credentials to be managed across their whole
  lifecycle.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: nist
    plain: >-
      Identities and credentials are expected to be issued, managed, rotated, and
      revoked, not left valid indefinitely.
    detail: >-
      NIST CSF 2.0 Access Control (PR.AA) covers managing identities, credentials,
      and their lifecycle.
    source: NIST CSF 2.0 · PR.AA
    sourceUrl: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Credentials are expected to be managed, and access removed promptly when it
      is no longer needed.
    detail: >-
      Maps to CC6.1, CC6.2, and CC6.3 (registering, managing, and removing access
      and credentials).
    source: SOC 2 TSC · CC6.1 / CC6.2 / CC6.3
    sourceUrl: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: iso27001
    plain: >-
      Authentication information and access rights are expected to be managed,
      including changing and revoking them appropriately.
    detail: >-
      Annex A controls A.5.17 (authentication information) and A.5.18 (access
      rights) in ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
    source: ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · A.5.17 / A.5.18
    sourceUrl: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
  - framework: pci-dss
    plain: >-
      Authentication credentials and cryptographic keys are expected to be changed
      and managed on a defined basis.
    detail: >-
      PCI DSS v4.0 Requirement 8 (authentication) and Requirement 3.6/3.7 (key
      management lifecycle).
    source: PCI DSS v4.0 · Req. 8 & 3.6
    sourceUrl: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/
---

**In practice.** Prefer short-lived, automatically issued credentials; rotate any
long-lived keys on a schedule; and revoke access immediately when someone leaves
or a service is retired.
