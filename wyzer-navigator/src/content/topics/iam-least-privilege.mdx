---
name: Least-Privilege Access
type: resource-linked
summary: >-
  Whether identities (people and services) are granted only the permissions they
  actually need. Frameworks consistently expect access to be scoped tightly and
  broad or standing administrative rights to be avoided.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: nist
    plain: >-
      Expects permissions to be managed on a least-privilege basis and separation
      of duties to be applied where appropriate.
    detail: >-
      NIST CSF 2.0 Identity Management, Authentication and Access Control (PR.AA)
      calls for access to be granted and enforced according to the principle of
      least privilege.
    citations:
      - label: "NIST CSF 2.0 · PR.AA"
        url: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Access is expected to be assigned based on role and need, and reviewed so
      that people and services do not accumulate excess permissions.
    detail: >-
      Maps to CC6.1 and CC6.3 in the Trust Services Criteria, granting, modifying,
      and removing access based on roles and least privilege.
    citations:
      - label: "SOC 2 TSC · CC6.1 / CC6.3"
        url: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: iso27001
    plain: >-
      Expects access rights to follow a defined access-control policy and to be
      limited to what each role requires.
    detail: >-
      Annex A controls A.5.15 (access control), A.5.18 (access rights), and A.8.2
      (privileged access rights) in ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
    citations:
      - label: "ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · A.5.15 / A.8.2"
        url: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
  - framework: hipaa
    plain: >-
      Expects access to health information to be limited to the minimum necessary,
      with each role given only the access its job requires.
    detail: >-
      The HIPAA Security Rule access-control standard and the minimum-necessary
      principle underpin this (45 CFR 164.312(a) and 164.502(b)).
    citations:
      - label: "HIPAA Security Rule · 164.312(a)"
        url: https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html
  - framework: pci-dss
    plain: >-
      Requires access to cardholder data and related systems to be restricted by
      business need-to-know and default-deny.
    detail: >-
      PCI DSS v4.0 Requirement 7 covers restricting access to system components and
      cardholder data by business need to know, defaulting to deny-all.
    citations:
      - label: "PCI DSS v4.0 · Requirement 7"
        url: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/
---

**In practice.** This means scoping roles narrowly, avoiding shared or blanket
admin accounts, granting time-bound elevation instead of standing privilege, and
reviewing access periodically to remove what is no longer needed.
