---
name: Access to Patient Records
type: industry-only
summary: >-
  Restricting who can view patient records. HIPAA expects access to health
  information to be limited to the minimum necessary and tied to each person's
  role.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: nist
    plain: >-
      Access to sensitive data is expected to follow least privilege, granted only
      to those who need it for their role.
    detail: >-
      NIST CSF 2.0 Access Control (PR.AA) covers enforcing least-privilege access
      to protected data.
    source: NIST CSF 2.0 · PR.AA
    sourceUrl: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Access to records is expected to be granted based on role and business need,
      and reviewed over time.
    detail: >-
      Maps to CC6.1 and CC6.3 (granting access based on roles and least
      privilege).
    source: SOC 2 TSC · CC6.1 / CC6.3
    sourceUrl: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: hipaa
    plain: >-
      Access to health information must be controlled and limited to the minimum
      necessary for each person's job.
    detail: >-
      HIPAA Security Rule access-control standard (45 CFR 164.312(a)) together with
      the minimum-necessary standard (164.502(b)).
    source: HIPAA Security Rule · 164.312(a)
    sourceUrl: https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/security/index.html
---

**In practice.** Use role-based access to records, give each person a unique user
ID, apply automatic logoff on shared devices, and keep an audit trail of who
viewed which record.
