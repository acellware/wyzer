---
name: Cardholder Data Encryption
type: industry-only
summary: >-
  How stored card numbers must be protected. PCI DSS expects the primary account
  number (PAN) to be rendered unreadable wherever it is stored.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: pci-dss
    plain: >-
      Wherever it is stored, the primary account number must be made unreadable,
      for example through strong encryption, truncation, tokenization, or hashing,
      and it must be masked when displayed.
    detail: >-
      PCI DSS v4.0 Requirement 3.4-3.5 covers rendering the PAN unreadable and
      masking it on display; Requirement 3.6-3.7 covers protecting and managing
      the cryptographic keys.
    source: PCI DSS v4.0 · Req. 3.4-3.7
    sourceUrl: https://www.pcisecuritystandards.org/
---

**In practice.** Tokenize or strongly encrypt the card number, show only a masked
version (such as the last four digits), and keep encryption keys managed
separately from the data they protect.
