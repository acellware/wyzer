---
name: Storing Personal Data (PII)
type: resource-linked
summary: >-
  Storing personal data about customers or users. Frameworks expect personal data
  to be collected minimally, kept only as long as needed, and protected
  throughout.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: nist
    plain: >-
      Data is expected to be protected across its lifecycle, matching safeguards
      to how sensitive it is.
    detail: >-
      NIST CSF 2.0 Data Security (PR.DS) covers protecting the confidentiality and
      integrity of stored data.
    citations:
      - label: "NIST CSF 2.0 · PR.DS"
        url: https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Personal and confidential information you commit to protect is expected to
      be safeguarded through its lifecycle.
    detail: >-
      Relates to CC6.1 and the Privacy criteria covering collection, retention,
      and disposal of personal information.
    citations:
      - label: "SOC 2 TSC · CC6.1 / Privacy"
        url: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: iso27001
    plain: >-
      Information, including personal data, is expected to be protected and its
      leakage prevented.
    detail: >-
      Annex A controls A.5.34 (privacy and protection of PII) and A.8.12 (data
      leakage prevention) in ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
    citations:
      - label: "ISO/IEC 27001:2022 · A.5.34 / A.8.12"
        url: https://www.iso.org/standard/27001
  - framework: gdpr
    plain: >-
      Collect only the personal data you need, keep it only as long as necessary,
      and protect it with appropriate measures.
    detail: >-
      GDPR Article 5 (data minimization and storage limitation) and Article 32
      (security of processing).
    citations:
      - label: "GDPR · Article 5 / Article 32"
        url: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
---

**In practice.** Minimize what you store, set retention limits and delete data
past them, restrict who can access it, and encrypt personal data at rest and in
transit.
