---
name: KYC Data Retention
type: industry-only
summary: >-
  How long identity-verification (KYC) records collected during onboarding are
  kept. Financial rules often require retaining them, while privacy law expects
  you not to keep them longer than necessary.
updated: 2026-09-04
cards:
  - framework: soc2
    plain: >-
      Personal and confidential onboarding information is expected to be handled,
      retained, and disposed of in line with your stated commitments and policies.
    detail: >-
      Relates to CC6.1 (protecting the data) and the Privacy criteria covering
      retention and disposal of personal information.
    source: SOC 2 TSC · CC6.1 / Privacy
    sourceUrl: https://www.aicpa-cima.com/topic/audit-assurance/audit-and-assurance-greater-than-soc-2
  - framework: gdpr
    plain: >-
      Identity data should be kept only as long as necessary and then deleted,
      balanced against any legal obligation to retain it, and secured throughout.
    detail: >-
      GDPR Article 5(1)(e) (storage limitation) and Article 32 (security of
      processing). Retention often interacts with financial-sector obligations
      outside these frameworks.
    source: GDPR · Art. 5(1)(e) / 32
    sourceUrl: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
---

**In practice.** Set a retention schedule that reflects your regulatory
obligations, restrict who can access KYC records, and delete or archive them once
the required period ends. Note that sector-specific anti-money-laundering rules,
which sit outside these frameworks, often set their own minimum retention.
